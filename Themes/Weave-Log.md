# Weave Log

Longitudinal log of patterns emerging from daily notes. Populated by `/weave` (runs automatically during `/weekly` when `next_weave` is due, or manually).

Each entry classifies patterns as:
- **Evolution** — was in the log before, has transformed
- **Weaving** — new, born from the combination of 2+ previous patterns
- **New** — discontinuous, not reducible to anything prior

Coverage matters more than depth: a pattern not recorded here disappears from longitudinal memory and risks being classified as New in future runs.
