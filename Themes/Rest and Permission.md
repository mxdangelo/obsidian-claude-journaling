# Rest and Permission

<!-- SAMPLE THEME NOTE — fictional persona "Alex". A theme note is distilled and
     updated by /weave, in prose, in the second person. Delete before you start. -->

A thread about treating rest as something to be earned rather than taken. It
surfaced first at the end of May: you noticed you fill your days so full there's
no room to think, then wonder why you feel behind — and that the day with a gap
in it was the one that felt lighter. Sam put the edge on it: "you keep treating
rest like a reward you have to earn."

Open question carried from the first weave: when you let a day have a gap in it,
what are you afraid will happen?
