# Direzione

*Not a list of goals. A compass.*

Copy this file to `Senso/Direzione.md` and rewrite it in your own voice. Update it when something shifts — not on a schedule.

---

## Who I want to be

*Few sentences in prose. The kind of person you recognize yourself moving toward. Not roles ("CEO", "father") but qualities of presence.*

## Where I want to be

*Concrete enough to imagine. Not "successful" — what does your day look like, who's nearby, what work fills your hours?*

## What sustains me

*The conditions that let the above be possible. Health, relationships, practices, money, time. Not a wishlist — the actual scaffolding.*

## What I'm leaving behind

*Old definitions of self that no longer fit. Recognize them so they don't pull you back unconsciously.*
